How to play
Cast with words

    
Three casts, one category each. Every answer you name sends a gust of wind that carries your line further out. Reel in at the end and see what bit.

    
    
      
  • Common answers are a breeze: +25 m. Uncommon is a wind: +45 m. Rare is a gale: +80 m.
    • 
      
  • Land 3 answers within 6 seconds to catch a tailwind: ×1.5 for 5 seconds.
    • 
      
  • You start with 20 seconds. Every correct answer adds time (2, 3 or 4 seconds by rarity, up to a full 20). Wrong or repeated answers tangle the line and cost time. When you run out of ideas, the line falls.
    • 
      
  • Further casts reach rarer fish: common, uncommon, rare, then legendary. Any cast can get a lucky bite, so keep filling your log book.
